## Solicitação

Create a handcrafted HD-2D pixel art sprite of a fantasy Mage for a modern JRPG.

Style: inspired by the visual quality of Sea of Stars, Chrono Trigger, Final Fantasy VI and The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap, while remaining original.

Character:
- Wise arcane mage
- Elegant blue and white robes with gold accents
- Long hooded cloak
- Wooden staff with a glowing blue crystal
- Leather belt with spell books and potion pouches
- Confident and calm expression
- Subtle magical aura
- Clean and iconic silhouette

Art Direction:
- Premium handcrafted pixel art
- HD-2D aesthetic
- Rich but controlled color palette
- Crisp pixel clusters
- Soft ambient lighting
- Light source from upper-left
- High readability
- Consistent proportions
- Transparent background
- Centered character
- Game-ready asset for Godot Engine
